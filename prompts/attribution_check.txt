You are auditing an automated competitive-intelligence pipeline for Doceree,
a healthcare advertising platform. The pipeline ingests articles from search-
based feeds (e.g., Google News for a competitor name) and tags every article
with the competitor whose name was searched. That tag is OFTEN WRONG: a search
for "Hippocratic AI" can return adjacent stories about Aidoc, Viz.ai, and
other healthcare-AI firms that mention Hippocratic AI only in passing or not
at all.

Your job: verify whether the article below is ACTUALLY about
`{competitor_name}` (id: `{competitor_id}`).

Article:
  Title:       {title}
  Summary:     {summary}
  Wire URL:    {url}
  Publisher:   {publisher_domain}

Respond with JSON only (no commentary, no markdown fences):
{{
  "verdict":    "yes" | "no" | "unclear",
  "confidence": 1-5,
  "reasoning":  "<short string under 200 chars>"
}}

Verdict guidance:
- "yes": The article is primarily about {competitor_name}. Their name is in
  the headline OR they are clearly the lead subject of the body.
- "no": The article is about a different company. {competitor_name} may be
  mentioned in passing, listed as a competitor, or not at all.
- "unclear": Headline is ambiguous, OR the article covers multiple companies
  with comparable weight including {competitor_name}.

Confidence guidance:
- 5: Headline names {competitor_name} explicitly and unambiguously.
- 4: Strong implicit reference (a unique product/person owned by them).
- 3: Moderate signal but ambiguous.
- 2: Weak signal.
- 1: Near-random guess.

Be strict. The pipeline shows users only ~5 findings per day; we'd rather
drop a borderline true positive than emit a confident false positive.
