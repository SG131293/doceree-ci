## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: Manual Prompt Pack
One Gemini AI Studio prompt template, run manually each morning.
- Effort: 1 day. Cost: ₹0/month. Completeness: 6/10.
- Pros: Ships immediately. Validates output design before automation. Zero infrastructure.
- Cons: 5-10 min/day manual work. No archive. No new-entrant discovery.

### Approach B: Single Apps Script
Apps Script + daily trigger + Gemini Flash/Pro + GPT-5.4-mini cross-check + HTML email + Drive archive.
- Effort: 5-7 days. Cost: ₹100-300/month. Completeness: 8/10.
- Pros: Automated, compounds via archive, free-tier-friendly.
- Cons: 6-min execution limit, single point of failure, prompts ship untested.

### Approach C: Manual-then-Automated Hybrid (RECOMMENDED)
Week 1: Approach A daily. Week 2-3: Approach B with prompts pressure-tested by week-1 use.
- Effort: 2-3 weeks. Cost: ₹100-300/month after week 2. Completeness: 9/10.
- Pros: De-risks build. Surfaces edge cases. Battle-tested prompts.
- Cons: Slower to "fully automated" by 1-2 weeks.
